The idea

From gaze to neurocinematics

We begin with eye tracking, then explore cinematic response through EEG, GSR, heart rate and behavioural measures.

World cinema

Bringing neurocinematics to world cinema

Neurocinematics has enormous potential for understanding cinema — but its application has largely focused on a limited range of filmmaking contexts. We want to broaden that picture.

By bringing films from under-represented countries, cultures and cinematic traditions into neurocinematic research, the project aims to create a more diverse understanding of how audiences experience film.

Three frames carry colour. The rest are the cinemas that neurocinematic research has yet to reach.
